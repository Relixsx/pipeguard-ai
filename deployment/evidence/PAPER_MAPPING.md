## Five executed components

1. Graph-preserving sensor selection and exact known-network immersion (D2).
2. External-reference two-stage module estimation with guarded attribution (D2; D1/D5 motivate assumptions and stress tests).
3. Target-specific identifiability and excitation placement (D4/D6).
4. Delayed spatial FIR source estimation, synchronized uploads and 684 recorded gas-waveform controlled-mixing transfer (D8; D7 motivates the branch).
5. Causal context-refinement gas detector with matched-data global ablation (D9).

The inventory, ARX, CNN–LSTM and residual hybrid remain inspectable baselines. Inventory balance is a conservation check, not a method attributed to Dankers.
