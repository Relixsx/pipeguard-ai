PROMISING, WITH ASSUMPTIONS

Mass balance catches the simulated leaks.

Broader calibration detected 36/36 injected events. The check uses the same known volumes and gas properties as the simulator. Its 0.245 false alarms/hour still exceeds the 0.1 calibration target.

A FAILURE WORTH KEEPING

Sensor drift needs its own check.

Mass balance alerted on 0/6 pressure-drift cases; the dynamic and hybrid checks alerted on 6/6. A single detector should not replace sensor-quality monitoring.

THE NEURAL TRADEOFF

A stricter threshold can hide leaks.

The CNN–LSTM's low false-alarm rate came with 0/36 detected events after broader calibration. The hybrid did not improve event recall over the dynamic predictor in this follow-up.

Only a new confirmed alarm at or after the injected onset earns detection credit. A pre-existing false alert receives no credit. Delays describe detected events only; missed events remain in recall. Confidence intervals are conditional on this simulator and event set.

Ideas translated into experiments.

Detailed paper mapping ↓
01

Model the connected process.

The dynamic predictor forecasts six measured physical channels using past pressures, flows, temperature and realized commands. The CNN–LSTM reconstructs all eight inputs; the hybrid monitors six whitened innovations.

D1 · D2 · D5The regularized ARX predictor is an engineering approximation. Feedback commands are not exogenous instruments; consistency is not proved.
02

Separate detection from attribution.

A detector can report an unexpected change while the physical cause remains ambiguous. This demo keeps leak location unestablished, even when the scenario's injected location is visible.

D3 · D4 · D6Finite-data regression diagnostics are not proofs of network identifiability. Quiet operation does not turn off anomaly detection.
03

Test context and the deployment boundary.

Healthy calibration includes a wider range of command changes. Every displayed score uses a trailing window. Missing sensors suspend scoring and restart warm-up; separate runs never share history.

D9 · D10These are adaptations of operating-context and forecasting lessons, not implementations of the papers' full proposed methods.
04

Earn the acoustic extension.

The real audio pilot tests dynamic and neural approaches on a small separate modality. A future source-separation study needs synchronized multiple microphones, propagation information and acquisition metadata.

D7 · D8Blind source separation and field localization are research directions. They are not implemented by this pressure/flow demo or its single-channel pilot.
